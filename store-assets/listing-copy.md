# Gold Ledger — Play Store listing copy

Paste these into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.

---

## App name (30 char limit)

```
Gold Ledger: Trading Sim
```

24 characters. The launcher label on the phone stays `Gold Ledger` — the two are
allowed to differ, and the longer store title is what makes the simulation explicit
to a reviewer scanning the listing.

---

## Short description (80 char limit)

```
Practice gold trading with virtual money. Risk-free simulator, no real funds.
```

77 characters. This is the line shown in search results, so the simulation framing
is deliberately in the first half.

## Full description (4000 char limit)

```
Gold Ledger is a gold trading simulator. Practice buying, selling and transferring
gold tokens in a live-feeling market — using virtual money, never real funds.

Every balance in Gold Ledger is simulated. There are no deposits, no withdrawals and
no payments of any kind. You cannot buy real gold, real currency or cryptocurrency
with this app, and you cannot lose real money using it. It is built for learning and
practice.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Start with a virtual balance and build a portfolio from scratch
• Buy and sell gold tokens at a simulated market price that moves in real time
• Transfer tokens to other Gold Ledger users by email
• Watch the market with a live price chart
• Track your portfolio value, profit and loss as prices move
• Review a complete history of every simulated trade you have made
• Top up your virtual balance whenever you want to start a new experiment

WHO IT IS FOR

Gold Ledger is for anyone who wants to understand how a trading interface works
before risking anything real — students, people new to markets, and anyone curious
about how buying and selling plays out over time. Because nothing is real, you can
make every mistake there is and simply reset.

HOW THE MARKET WORKS

The gold price in Gold Ledger is generated by the app. It moves continuously, drifts
and reverts the way a real instrument might, and is shared across all users so
everyone sees the same market. It is not a feed of real-world gold prices and should
never be used to inform a real financial decision.

PRIVACY

Gold Ledger asks only for a name, an email address and a password so your portfolio
persists between sessions. There is no advertising, no third-party tracking and no
analytics software in the app. You can permanently delete your account and all of its
data at any time from the profile drawer, without contacting anyone.

IMPORTANT

Gold Ledger is a simulation and an educational tool. It is not a financial product,
not an investment service, and not a broker. Nothing in the app is financial advice.
No real gold, money or cryptocurrency is ever bought, sold, held or transferred.
```

## Declarations these support

- **Financial features:** *My app doesn't provide any financial features.* True —
  no real transactions, no payment SDK in the dependency tree.
- **Ads:** No.
- **Target audience:** 18 and over. Avoids the Families policy programme entirely.
- **Content rating:** No wagering, no prizes, no simulated gambling. A trading
  simulator with no stake and no payout is not gambling, but answer the IARC
  questionnaire literally rather than by analogy.
