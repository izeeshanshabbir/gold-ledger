Gold Ledger is a gold trading simulator. Practice buying, selling and transferring gold tokens in a live-feeling market - using virtual money, never real funds.

Every balance in Gold Ledger is simulated. There are no deposits, no withdrawals and no payments of any kind. You cannot buy real gold, real currency or cryptocurrency with this app, and you cannot lose real money using it. It is built for learning and practice.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Start with a virtual balance and build a portfolio from scratch
- Buy and sell gold tokens at a simulated market price that moves in real time
- Transfer tokens to other Gold Ledger users by email
- Watch the market with a live price chart
- Track your portfolio value, profit and loss as prices move
- Review a complete history of every simulated trade you have made
- Top up your virtual balance whenever you want to start a new experiment

WHO IT IS FOR

Gold Ledger is for anyone who wants to understand how a trading interface works before risking anything real - students, people new to markets, and anyone curious about how buying and selling plays out over time. Because nothing is real, you can make every mistake there is and simply reset.

HOW THE MARKET WORKS

The gold price in Gold Ledger is generated by the app. It moves continuously, drifts and reverts the way a real instrument might, and is shared across all users so everyone sees the same market. It is not a feed of real-world gold prices and should never be used to inform a real financial decision.

PRIVACY

Gold Ledger asks only for a name, an email address and a password so your portfolio persists between sessions. There is no advertising, no third-party tracking and no analytics software in the app. You can permanently delete your account and all of its data at any time from the profile drawer, without contacting anyone.

IMPORTANT

Gold Ledger is a simulation and an educational tool. It is not a financial product, not an investment service, and not a broker. Nothing in the app is financial advice. No real gold, money or cryptocurrency is ever bought, sold, held or transferred.
