Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Gold Ledger account and its data at any time. You do not need to contact us first — it can be done inside the app in under a minute.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Gold Ledger and sign in.
  2. On the dashboard, tap your profile initial in the top-right corner to open the profile drawer.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation and tap Continue.
  5. Enter your password to confirm it is you, then tap Delete Forever.

Your account is removed immediately and you are returned to the login screen.

What gets deleted

  • Your login and password
  • Your name and email address
  • Your virtual cash and token balances
  • Your full history of simulated buys, sells and transfers

Nothing is retained on a delay and there is no recovery period — the deletion is immediate and permanent.

One thing that is not deleted

If another user sent you simulated tokens, a copy of that transfer stays in their transaction history, because it is part of their own records. That copy contains your account identifier and the email the transfer was sent to. We cannot remove it without deleting that other person’s data. Nothing else about you remains.

If you cannot sign in

If you have lost access to your account and cannot complete the steps above, email us from the address you registered with and we will delete the account for you.
